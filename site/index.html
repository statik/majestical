The problem

Your footage is on eleven drives in a drawer.

Every shoot ends the same way: cards offloaded to whichever drive had room, a folder name you meant to fix later, and a growing shelf of disks only you can navigate — until you can’t. The tools that offload carefully don’t search. The tools that search well want your library uploaded, indexed on someone else’s hardware, and rented back to you by the seat.

Majestical is built on a different assumption: the drives stay yours, the index stays on your Mac, and losing the app should never mean losing the work.

MM 3

What it does

Four things, done properly.

Proven intact

Ingest verifies every byte — source read, destination read-back, xxHash checksums — and writes industry-standard ASC MHL manifests that travel with the media. Chain of custody isn’t a paid tier.

Searchable on the shelf

The catalog keeps thumbnails, metadata, and checksums for every volume it has ever seen. Search a drive that’s in a drawer, in another city, or in a pelican case in someone’s trunk — and know exactly which disk to plug in.

Semantic search, at home

Image and keyframe embeddings run on your Mac’s neural engine. Type what you remember — “red boat at golden hour” — and get clips with timestamps. Point it at Ollama, LM Studio, or OpenRouter for captions and tag suggestions; each catalog chooses its own privacy line.

Team sync, no server

The catalog is an append-only event log that merges cleanly in any order. Sync it through a NAS folder, Dropbox, or the shuttle drive itself — plug in at the far end and both catalogs converge. Nobody runs infrastructure.

MM 2

Organization

Opinionated folders. Freeform tags.

Ingest files everything into a PARA layout — Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives — so the disk stays legible with or without the app. Everything else is folksonomy: tag what matters, in your own words, and the app suggests the vocabulary your team already uses instead of enforcing one.

Existing drives are cataloged exactly as they are. Nothing gets reorganized without you asking.

For people and agents

Everything the app can do, the terminal can do.

The GUI, the maj CLI, and a built-in MCP server share one engine. Ask Claude to find the selects, tag an ingest, or check a shelf of drives — every outcome a person can reach, an agent can reach, with destructive steps gated behind confirmation.
